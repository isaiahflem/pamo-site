Common questions

I didn't get my sign-up code

Check your spam or promotions folder for an email from noreply@pamo.site. On the code screen you can send a new one after a minute.

How do I join a club?

Open the Club tab, search for the club, and tap Join. Some clubs approve members first.

How do I report someone or something?

Tap ••• on the post, comment, photo, club, or profile and choose Report. You can block people the same way.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Settings (gear icon) → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and data.
